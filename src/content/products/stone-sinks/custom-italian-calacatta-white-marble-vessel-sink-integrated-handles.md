## Custom Italian Calacatta White Marble Vessel Sink with Integrated Handles

### A Bespoke Marble Basin Developed Around Your Design

This custom Italian Calacatta white marble vessel sink is developed for
customers seeking a distinctive natural stone bathroom fixture rather than
a standard off-the-shelf basin.

The integrated double-side handle silhouette, sculpted exterior and natural
marble veining give the basin a distinctive appearance. Instead of starting
with a fixed standard model, the design can be reviewed according to customer
drawings, reference images, dimensions and project requirements.

Stone selection, overall proportions, exterior details and surface finishing
can be coordinated before production. This approach is suitable for bespoke
bathroom interiors where the basin needs to complement a specific design
concept.

### Italian Calacatta Marble and Natural Stone Character

<a href="/materials/calacatta/">**Italian Calacatta white marble**</a> is valued for its light background and
distinctive natural veining. Because marble is a natural material, the
pattern and tonal variation differ between slabs.

For custom production, material selection should be discussed with reference
to the intended appearance, dimensions and fabrication requirements. Where
the project requires a particular vein direction or visual effect, slab
selection and layout can be reviewed before manufacturing.

### Integrated Handles and Sculpted Exterior

The integrated side handles are part of the overall basin design, rather than
separate decorative accessories. Their proportions and contours contribute
to the product's distinctive silhouette.

The sculpted exterior adds texture and depth to the stone body. Details such
as the handle profile, outer contours and decorative carving can be reviewed
against the customer's approved design.

The feasibility of each detail depends on the stone characteristics,
dimensions and fabrication requirements. Final drawings and specifications
should be confirmed before production.

### Crystal Surface Treatment for Marble

Natural marble requires appropriate care to preserve its appearance.
This basin uses a crystal surface treatment as an additional surface
finishing and protection process.

The treatment is intended to help improve surface wear resistance and
maintain the polished appearance over time. Actual results depend on the
marble, preparation, application process and subsequent maintenance.
Performance should not be interpreted as a guarantee that the stone is
scratch-proof, stain-proof or maintenance-free.

For projects with defined technical requirements, confirm the treatment
specification and request suitable test evidence before final approval.
Routine care should follow the recommended instructions for the selected
marble and surface treatment.
