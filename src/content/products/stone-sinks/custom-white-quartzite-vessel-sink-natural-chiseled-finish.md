## Product Overview

This **custom white quartzite vessel sink with natural chiseled finish** is designed for luxury bathrooms that require distinctive natural materials, sculptural form and carefully handcrafted details.

The sink is made from carefully selected **white crystal stone containing natural quartz components**, offering a hard stone structure together with a luminous white appearance and subtle natural mineral character.

Its most distinctive feature is the contrast between the refined basin interior and the naturally textured exterior. The outside surface is intentionally shaped using controlled hand-chiseling techniques to create an organic stone texture. After the natural texture is established, the surface is carefully refined to achieve a balanced appearance between raw stone character and precise craftsmanship.

The elongated racetrack-shaped design gives the basin a long, architectural profile that works particularly well with contemporary bathroom vanities and custom bathroom furniture.

Although the product is designed as a **single continuous basin**, its elongated proportions allow two faucets to be installed for simultaneous use. This makes it suitable for master bathrooms and double-user bathroom environments while maintaining the visual simplicity of one uninterrupted natural stone form.

As a **custom natural stone vessel sink**, the dimensions, basin proportions, faucet configuration, edge details and surface treatments can be adjusted according to individual project requirements.

## White quartzite Stone and Natural Chiseled Finish Details

The defining character of this bathroom sink comes from the combination of **white quartzite stone, handcrafted natural texture and refined basin geometry**.

The selected white crystal stone contains natural quartz components, contributing to the material's hard stone structure. The natural mineral composition also creates subtle variations in color, texture and visual depth, ensuring that each finished sink has its own individual character.

The exterior is not simply left unfinished.

Instead, the surface is carefully shaped through controlled hand-chiseling to reproduce an organic, naturally weathered stone appearance. The resulting texture creates irregular visual movement across the exterior while retaining the structural integrity required for a finished bathroom product.

After the chiseling process, the surface is refined and carefully finished so that the final product combines an authentic natural-stone appearance with professional fabrication quality.

The interior of the basin is shaped and finished to create a smoother surface suitable for everyday bathroom use.

This contrast between the **natural chiseled exterior and refined interior** gives the sink a strong handcrafted character and makes it particularly suitable for luxury bathroom interiors.

## Elongated Single Basin with Dual Faucet Configuration

One of the most practical features of this design is its elongated proportions.

The long racetrack-shaped form provides sufficient width and length for a **single continuous basin with two faucet positions**.

This allows two users to share the same sink comfortably while maintaining a visually clean and sculptural stone form.

Unlike a conventional double vanity sink that uses two separate bowls, this design maintains one continuous natural stone basin.

The configuration can be adapted according to the bathroom layout and plumbing requirements.

For master bathrooms, luxury residential developments and hospitality projects, the dual-faucet configuration can provide a practical solution for shared bathroom use while preserving the distinctive appearance of a sculptural natural stone sink.
