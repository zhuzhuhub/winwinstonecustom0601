## Natural White Jade

Natural white jade provides the defining character of this vessel sink.

Unlike uniformly manufactured surfaces, natural jade contains variations in
color, translucency, mineral structure and internal pattern. These natural
characteristics give the finished sink a depth that changes subtly under
different lighting conditions.

The predominantly white stone creates a clean and sophisticated appearance,
while the natural mineral lines introduce visual movement across the basin.

The result is a material-driven design in which the natural stone itself
becomes the main decorative element.

Because natural jade is a geological material, the exact color, veining,
translucency and mineral pattern will vary from one stone selection to
another.

---

## Translucent Stone Character

One of the most distinctive characteristics of this natural white jade sink
is its ability to transmit light under suitable conditions.

When illuminated from an appropriate direction, areas of the stone can reveal
a soft translucent effect, allowing the internal structure and natural
variation of the material to become more visible.

This characteristic gives the sink a different visual quality from
conventional opaque marble or engineered stone surfaces.

For interior designers and architects, the translucent nature of the
material can also provide opportunities for creative lighting concepts,
depending on the stone thickness, lighting arrangement and final installation
requirements.

The exact degree of light transmission varies naturally according to the
selected jade stone and its thickness.

The basin features a simple rectangular form with gently softened edges.

## Natural Veining and Mineral Variation

Natural mineral patterns are an important part of the visual identity of this
white jade vessel sink.

Subtle lines, cloudy variations and darker mineral traces move through the
stone, creating a pattern that cannot be reproduced exactly from one piece to
another.

These natural variations mean that every completed sink has its own
individual appearance.

For projects requiring multiple sinks, stone selection and layout can be
discussed in advance to achieve a more consistent overall visual direction
while preserving the natural character of the material.

## Surface Finish Options

The natural white jade can be supplied with different surface treatments
according to the desired appearance and project requirements.

### Polished Finish

A polished finish creates a smooth reflective surface and enhances the
luminous character of the white jade.

It can also emphasize the natural mineral variation and translucency of the
stone, making it particularly suitable for luxury bathroom interiors.

### Honed Finish

A honed finish creates a softer, lower-sheen appearance while maintaining
the natural color and character of the jade.

This option can be considered for interiors where a more understated natural
stone appearance is preferred.

### Custom Finish

Custom surface treatments can be discussed according to the selected jade,
design requirements, installation environment and intended aesthetic.

The final finish should always be evaluated against the specific stone slab
because natural jade can respond differently to surface treatments depending
on its mineral composition and structure.
