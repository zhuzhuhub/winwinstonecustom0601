## Natural Grey Travertine with Distinctive Layered Veining

The main material of this bathtub is natural grey travertine.

Travertine develops through natural mineral deposition, creating distinctive formations, layered patterns and tonal variations. In this design, the horizontal mineral formations create a flowing visual effect across the curved bathtub walls.

The grey and earthy color palette offers a softer alternative to high-contrast marble, making the material suitable for interiors that favor natural textures and a restrained atmosphere.

Natural stone characteristics may vary in:

- Grey and taupe color balance
- Horizontal mineral formations
- Veining and pattern distribution
- Natural surface texture
- Overall tonal variation

Each bathtub therefore has its own natural appearance. Material selection can be discussed before production to help achieve the intended design direction.

## Filled Travertine Surface Treatment for Practical Bathroom Use

Natural travertine is characterized by its natural pores and cavities. These formations contribute to the material's distinctive appearance, but open cavities can also make routine surface cleaning less convenient in certain applications.

For this bathtub, pore-filling treatment was selected according to the customer's practical usage requirements.

The filling process addresses exposed cavities to create a more continuous surface while preserving the natural stone's overall color and layered character.

The main benefits include:

- Reducing exposed natural cavities
- Creating a more continuous surface
- Making routine surface cleaning more practical
- Supporting easier day-to-day bathroom maintenance
- Retaining the natural visual character of travertine

The treatment is intended to improve surface practicality rather than make natural stone maintenance-free. Appropriate stone-care products and regular maintenance are still recommended.
