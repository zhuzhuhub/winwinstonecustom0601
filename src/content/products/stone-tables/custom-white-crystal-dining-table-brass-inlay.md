## Product Overview

This **custom white crystal dining table with brass inlay** is designed as
a distinctive statement piece for luxury residential, hospitality and
high-end commercial interiors.

The design combines the natural visual character of white crystal with
precisely integrated brass lines, creating a refined contrast between
the luminous stone surface and warm metallic details. The result is a
sculptural dining table with a strong architectural presence and a
highly individual appearance.

Unlike mass-produced furniture, every **custom white crystal dining
table** can be developed according to specific project requirements.
Dimensions, tabletop proportions, crystal selection, brass inlay patterns
and structural details can all be adjusted to suit the intended space.

The natural mineral characteristics of white crystal make every piece
visually unique. Variations in texture, translucency and internal
patterns contribute to the individuality and luxury character of the
finished table.

This bespoke crystal dining table is suitable for luxury villas, private
residences, boutique hotels, restaurants and other interior projects
where premium materials and custom furniture design are required.

## White Crystal and Brass Inlay Details

The defining feature of this dining table is the combination of
**white crystal stone and brass inlay**.

The white crystal surface creates a soft, luminous appearance with
naturally occurring mineral textures and subtle visual depth. Depending
on the selected material slab, each tabletop may feature different
patterns, translucency and tonal variations.

Precision brass lines are carefully integrated into the tabletop to
create a geometric decorative composition. The warm metallic appearance
of brass provides an elegant contrast against the lighter crystal
surface while emphasizing the custom design of the table.

The brass inlay pattern can also be customized according to the design
requirements of the project.

Customization options may include:

- Straight brass inlay lines
- Geometric brass patterns
- Custom decorative layouts
- Different line widths
- Custom tabletop compositions
- Project-specific design details

This combination of natural crystal and metallic inlay makes the table
particularly suitable for interiors seeking a distinctive luxury focal
point.
