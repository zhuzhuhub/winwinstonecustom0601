## Product Overview

This **custom red travertine oval dining table with cylindrical stone legs** is designed as a distinctive yet refined statement piece for luxury residential, hospitality and high-end commercial interiors.

The design combines a warm red travertine tabletop with an elongated oval profile and two substantial cylindrical natural stone legs. The result is a contemporary **natural stone dining table** that emphasizes material character, clean proportions and understated architectural design.

The travertine tabletop features naturally occurring horizontal textures and warm red-brown tonal variations. These natural characteristics give the stone surface depth and individuality, ensuring that each finished dining table has its own visual character.

The elongated oval, or racetrack-shaped, tabletop provides generous dining space while maintaining a soft and balanced silhouette. Rounded ends help create a more approachable profile and work well in contemporary, modern, minimalist and luxury dining environments.

The cylindrical stone legs provide a simple structural solution that complements the tabletop without competing with its natural texture. Their substantial proportions reinforce the solid character of the travertine while maintaining a clean and minimalist appearance.

As a **custom travertine dining table**, the design can be adapted according to individual project requirements. Dimensions, tabletop proportions, stone selection, edge profiles, leg diameter, leg placement and finishing details can all be customized.

## Red Travertine Tabletop and Cylindrical Leg Details

The defining character of this dining table comes from the combination of **red travertine, an elongated oval tabletop and simple cylindrical stone legs**.

The tabletop uses a softly rounded profile with curved ends and a carefully finished edge. This elongated shape provides practical dining space while creating a visually balanced silhouette.

Red travertine offers a warmer and more distinctive alternative to conventional beige or ivory stone surfaces. The material shown in this design features warm red-brown tones with natural horizontal movement and subtle variations across the slab.

As with other natural stone products, the final appearance of each tabletop can vary depending on the selected slab. Differences in color, texture, mineral movement and natural pattern are inherent characteristics of genuine travertine.

The cylindrical legs are intentionally simple in form. Their rounded geometry creates a strong contrast with the broad horizontal tabletop while maintaining visual consistency with the natural stone material.

The legs can also be adjusted according to the requirements of different projects.

- Red travertine slab selection
- Custom cylindrical leg diameter

- Alternative stone base designs
- Custom surface finishes
- Project-specific structural details

This combination of warm natural stone and simple geometric forms makes the table suitable for interiors where material quality and architectural simplicity are central to the design.
