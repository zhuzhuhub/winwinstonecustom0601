## Custom Italian White Marble Round Dining Table

This custom round dining table combines natural Italian white marble with a
distinctive two-tier circular tabletop and a sculpted stone base.

The raised upper section is designed to rotate manually, adding a practical
function to the refined circular composition. Beneath the tabletop, the
sculpted stone supports create a strong architectural presence through
curved carved forms and substantial natural stone construction.

The predominantly white marble surface is naturally marked by delicate grey
web-like veining, giving each table a unique appearance while maintaining a
clean and sophisticated aesthetic.

---

## Natural Italian White Marble

The table is crafted from natural white marble sourced from Italy.

Its predominantly white background provides a bright and refined appearance,
while the natural grey veining creates subtle movement across the surface.

The veining forms an intricate, web-like pattern that varies naturally from
slab to slab. As a result, every finished dining table has its own individual
combination of mineral movement, grey tones and natural stone character.

The marble can be selected according to the desired level of veining,
background whiteness and overall visual balance.

## Two-Tier Circular Tabletop

The defining feature of this dining table is its two-tier circular tabletop.

The main circular surface provides the primary dining area, while a smaller
raised circular section sits at the center.

The two concentric forms create a subtle sense of depth and layering while
maintaining the visual balance of the round dining table.

The proportions of the main tabletop and raised center can be customized
according to the required table diameter, seating capacity and overall
design concept.

---

## Manually Rotating Center

The upper circular section is designed to rotate manually.

This integrated rotating center provides a practical dining function,
allowing shared dishes, serving pieces and tableware to be moved more easily
between diners.

Rather than appearing as a separate accessory, the rotating section is
integrated into the overall stone tabletop design, maintaining a clean and
coordinated appearance.

The diameter, thickness and proportions of the rotating center can be
customized according to the overall table design.

---

## Sculpted Solid-Stone Base

The base is one of the most distinctive elements of this marble dining table.

The supporting structure uses substantial stone forms that are sculpted into
curved architectural shapes rather than relying on conventional table legs.

The carved openings create a sense of lightness within the substantial stone
structure, while the continuous curved surfaces give the base a strong
monolithic character.

This approach allows the natural marble veining to continue across the
supporting elements, making the table visually cohesive from top to base.

---

## Natural Grey Veining as a Design Element

The natural grey veining is an important part of the table's visual identity.

Against the white marble background, the delicate grey patterns create a
subtle contrast without overpowering the overall design.

The web-like mineral movement works particularly well with contemporary,
minimalist, modern luxury and refined residential interiors.

Because natural stone is inherently variable, the final veining pattern
cannot be completely duplicated.

For custom projects, slab selection can be discussed to achieve a preferred
balance between white background, grey movement and overall pattern density.

---

## Surface Finish Options

The natural Italian white marble can be produced with different surface
treatments depending on the desired appearance and project requirements.

### Polished Finish

A polished finish enhances the brightness of the white marble and increases
the visual clarity of the natural grey veining.

It creates a refined and luminous appearance suitable for luxury residences,
contemporary dining rooms, hospitality interiors and high-end furniture
projects.

### Honed Finish

A honed finish provides a softer and more understated surface with reduced
reflection.

This treatment emphasizes the tactile character of the natural stone and
works particularly well with minimalist, contemporary and organic-modern
interiors.

### Custom Finish

Custom surface treatments can also be discussed according to the selected
marble slab, design concept and intended application.

Final finish selection can be confirmed during the material selection and
production process.
