## Custom Cream Travertine Round Low Console Table

This custom cream travertine round low console table combines the natural warmth of travertine with a simple, sculptural furniture design.

The table features a large circular tabletop supported by three rounded cylindrical stone legs. Its low profile, generous tabletop and substantial stone supports create a balanced composition that works as both a functional table and a natural stone design feature.

A key detail of this piece is its specially treated tabletop. Natural travertine contains pores and cavities, which can make an untreated surface more difficult to clean and maintain. To address this characteristic, the tabletop has been filled and treated with resin according to the customer's requirements.

The treatment helps create a smoother and more practical surface while retaining the natural cream tone and distinctive texture of travertine.

The featured design can be customized for different interior spaces and furniture projects.

## Filled and Resin-Treated Travertine Tabletop

Natural travertine is characterized by its pores, cavities and naturally formed surface variations. These features contribute to its distinctive appearance, but open pores can also make a tabletop more difficult to clean.

For this project, the tabletop has been specially processed according to the customer's requirements.

### Travertine Pore Filling

The natural pores and cavities are filled to create a more even surface. This helps reduce the open-pore texture and makes routine surface cleaning more convenient.

### Resin Surface Treatment

A resin treatment is applied to the tabletop to provide a smoother finished surface. It helps improve the everyday practicality of travertine when used as a furniture tabletop.

### Retaining Natural Stone Character

The purpose of the treatment is not to hide the identity of the material. The warm cream color and natural travertine patterns remain important visual features of the finished table.

The final surface appearance can vary depending on the selected stone, filling material and resin treatment specifications. Samples and finishing expectations should be confirmed before production.
