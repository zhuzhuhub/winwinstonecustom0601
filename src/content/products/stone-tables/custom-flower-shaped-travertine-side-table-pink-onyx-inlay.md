## Custom Flower-Shaped Natural Stone Side Table

This custom flower-shaped natural stone side table combines sculptural stone carving with decorative natural stone inlay.

The design features an organic flower-shaped tabletop, with individual petal sections created from natural pink onyx and framed by cream travertine. Beneath the tabletop, a sculpted travertine pedestal base continues the natural, flowing character of the overall design.

Unlike conventional round or rectangular stone side tables, this piece uses the shape and arrangement of natural stone to create a distinctive floral furniture design.

The combination of warm cream travertine and soft pink onyx gives the table a decorative appearance while allowing the natural textures of both materials to remain visible.

The featured color combination is only one design possibility. Different stone colors and materials can be selected according to individual preferences.

The tabletop is the defining feature of this custom stone side table.

Its outline follows an organic flower-inspired shape rather than a conventional geometric tabletop. The surface is divided into individual petal sections, with natural pink onyx inlaid into the travertine structure.

Each section contributes to the overall floral pattern, creating a layered visual effect through the contrast between the light travertine frame and the colored natural stone inlays.

The design combines several fabrication details:

- Custom flower-shaped tabletop cutting
- Individual petal-shaped stone sections
- Natural stone inlay detailing
- Carefully shaped connecting borders
- Custom edge profiling
- Surface finishing and polishing

The exact petal arrangement and stone proportions can be adjusted according to the approved design.
