## Beige Travertine and Copper Accent Details

The defining character of this coffee table comes from the combination of **beige travertine and warm copper accents**.

Beige travertine is valued for its naturally warm neutral color, distinctive linear texture and organic stone character. The material works particularly well in contemporary, modern, minimalist and luxury interior environments where natural materials are used to create a calm and sophisticated atmosphere.

The selected travertine can contain natural variations in tone, texture and surface movement. These variations are an inherent characteristic of natural stone and contribute to the individuality of each finished coffee table.

Copper-colored metal accents are incorporated into the table structure to introduce a subtle metallic contrast. The warm metallic tone complements the beige travertine rather than overpowering the natural stone, creating a balanced combination of stone and metal.

Depending on the project requirements, the metal detailing can be customized to coordinate with other interior elements.

Customization options may include:

- Copper-colored metal accents
- Alternative metal finishes
- Custom accent shapes
- Different accent proportions
- Project-specific metal detailing
- Custom stone and metal combinations

This combination of natural travertine and warm metal detailing makes the table suitable for interiors seeking a sophisticated yet understated focal point.
