## Custom White Marble Dining Table with Pink Veining

This custom marble dining table combines a bright natural white marble
surface with distinctive pink and warm-toned veining, creating a refined
statement piece for contemporary interiors.

The elongated racetrack-shaped tabletop is paired with a two-part geometric
stone base, using simple slab construction to create a sculptural yet
restrained architectural form.

The contrast between the clean white background and naturally occurring
pink mineral movement gives the table a subtle fashion-forward character,
making it suitable for interiors that require a sophisticated touch of
color without relying on artificial decorative elements.

---

## Natural White Marble with Pink Veining

The tabletop is crafted from natural white marble characterized by a light
background and distinctive pink, rose-toned and warm mineral veining.

The natural color variation gives the marble a visually expressive
appearance while retaining the clean and sophisticated character associated
with white natural stone.

Rather than applying color as a decorative treatment, the pink tones are
part of the stone's natural mineral pattern.

Because natural marble varies from slab to slab, every completed dining
table has its own unique combination of veining, tonal variation and mineral
movement.

## Elongated Racetrack-Shaped Tabletop

The tabletop uses an elongated racetrack profile, combining the generous
length of a rectangular dining table with the softer visual character of
rounded ends.

The continuous curved ends create a smooth silhouette around the dining
space while eliminating sharp corners from the main tabletop profile.

This shape works particularly well in contemporary dining rooms where a
large dining surface is required without introducing a visually heavy
rectangular form.

Table length, width, thickness and edge treatment can be customized
according to seating capacity and project requirements.

---

## Two-Part Geometric Stone Base

One of the defining features of this dining table is its two-part stone
base.

Instead of using conventional four-leg construction, the table uses
geometric slab elements assembled into two substantial support sections.

The simple arrangement creates a strong architectural presence while
maintaining the clean visual language of the tabletop.

The combination of vertical slabs, curved transitions and open spaces gives
the base a sculptural quality without excessive decoration.

---

## Simple Geometric Construction

The supporting structure is intentionally based on simple geometric forms.

Individual stone elements are arranged to create a balanced composition,
allowing the natural marble pattern to remain visible across both the
tabletop and supporting structure.

This approach creates a contemporary stone dining table that can work across
a range of interior styles, from modern luxury and minimalist spaces to
organic contemporary and sophisticated residential interiors.

The proportions, spacing and geometry of the support structure can be
adjusted for different table sizes and design concepts.

---

## Pink Veining as a Design Element

The natural pink veining gives this white marble dining table a distinctive
visual identity.

The warm pink and subtle brown-grey mineral movement introduces color and
visual energy into the predominantly white surface, allowing the table to
become a focal point within a relatively restrained interior.

This makes the design particularly suitable for contemporary spaces where
natural stone is used not only as a functional material but also as a
decorative architectural element.

The final appearance depends on the selected slab, allowing clients to
choose different levels of veining intensity and pattern according to the
overall interior concept.

## Surface Finish Options

The natural white marble can be supplied with different surface treatments
depending on the desired appearance and application.

### Polished Finish

A polished finish enhances the brightness of the white marble and brings
greater visual clarity to the natural pink and warm-toned veining.

It creates a refined and luminous appearance suitable for luxury residential
interiors, contemporary dining rooms and high-end hospitality projects.

### Honed Finish

A honed finish provides a softer, more understated surface with reduced
reflection.

This treatment emphasizes the tactile character of the natural stone and
works well with contemporary, minimalist and organic-modern interiors.

### Custom Finish

Custom surface treatments can also be developed according to the selected
marble slab, design requirements and intended application.

Final finish selection can be discussed during the material and production
process.
