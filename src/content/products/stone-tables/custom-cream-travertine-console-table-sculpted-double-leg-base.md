## Natural Cream Travertine

Cream travertine is particularly well suited to contemporary and
organic-modern interiors because of its soft cream and warm neutral
color palette.

Unlike completely uniform manufactured surfaces, natural travertine
contains organic pores, subtle tonal variation and naturally occurring
surface movement. These characteristics give each finished piece a
unique appearance.

On this console table, the soft cream tone creates a calm visual
foundation while the natural porous texture adds depth and tactile
interest.

## Surface Finish Options

Cream travertine offers two distinctive surface treatments, allowing
the natural character of the stone to be presented in either a refined
or more organic form.

### Filled Finish

A filled finish treats the natural pores and voids of the travertine
by filling them before the final surface treatment. This creates a
more uniform and refined surface while retaining the characteristic
color variation and natural movement of the stone.

The filled finish is particularly suitable for contemporary interiors
where a cleaner tabletop appearance and easier daily maintenance are
preferred.

### Natural / Unfilled Finish

The natural or unfilled finish preserves the natural pores, cavities
and surface texture of the travertine. Instead of filling the
characteristic openings of the stone, this treatment allows its
original geological character to remain visible.

The result is a more organic and tactile appearance, making the
console table particularly suitable for natural, Mediterranean,
Japandi, rustic-modern and organic contemporary interiors.

Both finish options can be discussed according to the selected
travertine slab, design requirements and intended application.
