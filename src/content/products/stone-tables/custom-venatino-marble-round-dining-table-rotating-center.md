## Custom Venatino Marble Round Dining Table

This custom round dining table combines natural Venatino marble with a
distinctive two-tier circular tabletop and a manually rotating upper center.

The combination of a bright white marble surface, delicate grey natural
veining and sculptural stone legs creates a contemporary dining table with
both functional and architectural character.

Designed for luxury residences, private dining spaces, hospitality projects
and high-end interior developments, the table can be customized in size,
stone selection, base geometry and surface finish.

---

## Natural Venatino Marble

The table is crafted from natural Venatino marble, a white marble
characterized by a light background and delicate grey veining.

The subtle grey movement provides visual depth across the otherwise clean
white surface, allowing the natural stone to become the primary decorative
element of the dining table.

Because natural marble varies from slab to slab, every finished tabletop
has its own individual pattern, mineral movement and tonal variation.

## Two-Tier Circular Tabletop

The most distinctive feature of this dining table is its two-level circular
tabletop.

The main dining surface forms a broad round platform, while a smaller raised
circular section is positioned at the center.

This layered configuration creates a subtle architectural effect while
maintaining the visual simplicity of the round dining table.

The proportions of both circular sections can be adjusted according to the
overall diameter, seating requirements and design concept.

---

## Manually Rotating Center

The raised upper circular section is designed to rotate manually.

This integrated rotating center provides a practical dining function,
allowing shared dishes, serving pieces and tableware to be moved more easily
between diners.

Unlike a separate tabletop accessory, the rotating element is incorporated
into the overall design of the stone dining table, creating a visually
integrated and refined appearance.

This feature can be customized according to the required diameter,
mechanism configuration and tabletop proportions.

---

## Sculptural Three-Branch Stone Base

The dining table is supported by a distinctive sculptural stone base with a
three-branch configuration.

Rather than using conventional cylindrical or four-leg construction, the
stone supports are shaped as substantial architectural elements that create
a strong visual connection between the tabletop and the floor.

The raw-stone-inspired geometry gives the base a monolithic appearance while
the curved and softened edges keep the overall form contemporary and refined.

The number, proportions, spacing and geometry of the supporting elements can
be developed according to the requirements of each custom project.

---

## Natural Grey Veining as a Design Element

The natural grey veining of Venatino marble creates subtle movement across
the circular tabletop.

Against the predominantly white background, the fine grey patterns provide
a restrained contrast that works well with contemporary, minimalist,
Japandi, modern luxury and sophisticated residential interiors.

The natural variation of the marble also means that each completed table has
a unique surface pattern.

Vein direction, slab selection and visual balance can be discussed during the
material selection and production process.

---

## Surface Finish Options

The natural Venatino marble can be produced with different surface
treatments depending on the desired appearance and project requirements.

### Polished Finish

A polished finish enhances the brightness of the white marble and increases
the visual clarity of the natural grey veining.

It creates a refined, luminous appearance suitable for luxury residential
interiors, contemporary dining rooms and high-end hospitality projects.

### Honed Finish

A honed finish creates a softer, more understated surface with reduced
reflection.

This option is suitable for interiors where a contemporary matte appearance
and more tactile natural-stone character are preferred.

### Custom Finish

Custom surface treatments can also be discussed according to the selected
stone slab, design concept and project requirements.

Final finish selection should be confirmed according to the intended
application and maintenance requirements.
