## Project-Based Custom Stone Furniture for Commercial Interiors

This custom stone reception desk and bar counter is designed for projects
where standard furniture cannot meet the required dimensions, shape,
material or architectural design.

Unlike a conventional stone table, this product is developed as a
**project-based custom stone furniture solution**.
The overall design can be developed according to project drawings,
architectural requirements, target dimensions, functional requirements and
installation conditions.

For large reception desks, the approved design can also be optimized before
production and divided into several independent modules suitable for
international transportation.

This allows more of the precision stone fabrication, finishing and quality
control work to be completed at the factory, while simplifying the final
assembly process at the project site.

Typical applications include:
- Hotel reception desks
- Hotel lobby counters
- Resort reception counters
- Office reception desks
- Commercial reception areas
- Restaurant and hospitality counters
- Retail and showroom interiors
- Custom stone bar counters
- Bespoke commercial interiors
- Architectural stone furniture projects

## Designed for International Transportation and On-Site Assembly

Large stone reception desks can present a practical transportation and
installation challenge.
A design that works well as one complete piece may be difficult to ship
internationally because of its overall dimensions, weight and handling
requirements.

For large custom reception desks, we can review the approved design before
production and optimize the overall structure into several independent
modules suitable for international transportation.

The number, size and configuration of the modules are determined according
to the:
- Overall project dimensions
- Stone construction
- Structural requirements
- Connection points
- Transportation limitations
- Packing requirements
- Site access conditions
- Final installation method

Each module is then precision fabricated and finished at the factory.
Where appropriate, the modules can also be dry-fitted or checked before
packing to verify dimensions, alignment, joints and overall appearance.

After international transportation, the modules can be delivered to the
project site and connected or assembled together to recreate the complete
reception desk.

This modular fabrication approach can help:
- Make large stone reception desks more suitable for international shipping
- Reduce transportation difficulties caused by oversized components
- Minimize complex stone fabrication work at the project site
- Improve installation efficiency
- Move more precision fabrication into the factory
- Allow factory quality control before shipment
- Maintain the intended appearance of the approved design

The exact modular configuration is developed for each project rather than
using a fixed standard structure.

## Factory Fabrication, Modular Assembly and Quality Control

For large custom stone reception desks, production is planned with both
fabrication and transportation in mind.
Before manufacturing begins, the approved design can be reviewed and
optimized into transportable modules.
The stone components are then precision fabricated according to the approved
dimensions and construction details.

Depending on the project, factory processes may include:
- Stone cutting
- CNC or precision shaping
- Curved stone fabrication
- Edge profiling
- Surface finishing
- Component fitting
- Dry fitting
- Dimensional inspection
- Joint and alignment checking
- Final quality inspection

For suitable designs, individual modules can be assembled or dry-fitted at
the factory before packing.

This allows potential dimensional or fitting issues to be identified before
international shipment rather than during final installation.

After the modules arrive at the project site, they can be positioned,
connected and assembled according to the approved installation plan.
